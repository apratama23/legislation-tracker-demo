      Categories

      A bill is put in every category with a keyword in its name. Upper and lower case are the same. A keyword must start a word: one of four letters or fewer must be the whole word, and a longer one also finds words it begins. Several words are matched as a phrase. A change applies to every bill at once, on every page, except bills an administrator has set by hand.

      Add a category

      Every change to a category is listed under History.
