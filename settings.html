      When it may send

      Local time, on a 24-hour clock. Grace is how late the digest may still go out if the system was down at its usual time; after that it is skipped rather than sent stale.

      Every alert

      Everyone listed receives every alert, whatever its category. Urgent movements arrive on their own; everything else is gathered into one digest each weekday morning.

      In the real system, changing these changes what journalists receive. Here nothing is sent.

      Emails by category

      Followers of a category receive only the bills in it. A bill in two categories reaches the followers of both, once each. Open a category to change who follows it.

      One address per line. A category may have nobody.
